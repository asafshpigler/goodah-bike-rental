From One Ride to a Better Future

In April 2025, a simple idea became reality. Together with Goodah Platform and Wonderland Siquijor, we launched a scooter rental where 100% of the profits support Bata ng Calabnugan Inc. - a home for orphaned and at-risk children.

Why this matters

This is not a one-time donation. It’s an income-generating asset.
One scooter can generate hundreds of dollars every month — meaning thousands of dollars supporting children over time, from a single scooter.
